Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 27, 2026  ·  Effective upon full platform launch

1. Introduction

The Peripheral Initiative ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what rights you have over it.

By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Platform.

Our core data pledge: We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not allow advertisers to target you based on your activity on this platform. Your data is used to operate the platform and keep it safe — nothing else.

2.3 Information Stored Locally (Your Device Only)

The following data is stored in your browser's local storage and remains on your device. We do not have access to it from our servers:

  • Your vote history (upvotes/downvotes on posts and comments)
  • Your saved posts
  • Your privacy and notification preferences
  • Your session authentication state

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account management: Creating, maintaining, and securing your account
  • Platform operation: Displaying posts, comments, and profiles; enabling forum interaction
  • Safety and moderation: Detecting and acting on violations of our Terms of Service, including spam, harassment, and ban evasion
  • Communications: Sending account-related notifications (verification, security alerts, moderation actions)
  • Platform improvement: Understanding how the platform is used to make it better
  • Legal compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable law, including DMCA takedown processing

We do not use your data for advertising, selling to data brokers, or any purpose unrelated to operating this platform.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Third-party vendors who assist in operating the platform (e.g., hosting providers, email delivery services) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Payment processors: If you make a purchase, your payment information is handled directly by our PCI-compliant payment provider and is never stored by us
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of The Peripheral Initiative, its users, or the public
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may transfer to the new entity, with notice provided to users

Your username and public posts are visible to all platform visitors by default, unless you adjust your privacy settings to limit post visibility.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to certain uses of your data
  • Right to restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at lachlan@theperipheralinitiative.com. We will respond within 30 days. Account deletion can also be initiated directly through your account Settings at any time.

Note: Users in the European Union or California may have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively. Full regional compliance details will be addressed in the legally reviewed version of this Policy.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform uses browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to maintain your session state, preferences, and locally stored activity such as votes and saves. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

We may use session cookies for security purposes (e.g., CSRF protection). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that report your behavior to external services.

You can clear your local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will sign you out and reset your locally stored preferences.

8. Third-Party Services

The Platform currently uses or may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Google may collect basic request data per their privacy policy
  • Payment processor (future): A PCI-compliant payment provider (such as Stripe) will handle payment data directly — we will not store card numbers
  • Discogs API (associated application): Used to retrieve music release data for the Dis-GO app. No personal data is shared with Discogs

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. We encourage you to review their policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user under 18 has provided personal information, we will delete that information and terminate the associated account immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 18 has registered on the Platform, please contact us at lachlan@theperipheralinitiative.com immediately.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Hashed storage of sensitive verification data (passwords and answer keys are never stored in plaintext)
  • Access controls limiting who within our organization can access personal data
  • Regular review of our security practices

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by posting a notice on the Platform or via email. The date at the top of this document reflects the most recent update. Your continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
