1. Introduction

The Peripheral Initiative ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what rights you have over it.

Scope. This Policy covers the Peripheral Initiative website and forum, and Dis-Go, our mobile application for discovering physical music. Dis-Go collects a different and more sensitive set of information than the website — particularly location and camera data — and those differences are set out explicitly in Section 3 rather than buried in general language.Updated

By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Platform.

Our core data pledge: We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not allow advertisers to target you based on your activity. Your data is used to operate the platform and keep it safe — nothing else.

4. Location dataNew

Location is the most sensitive information Dis-Go handles, so it gets its own section.

  • You choose the permission. The app asks explicitly, and you can select “While Using the App,” “Always Allow,” or “Not Now.” There is no silent background tracking.
  • Precise location is never shown to another user. Not in your profile, not on a leaderboard, not in a shop thread, and not through any interface another person can reach.
  • Your check-in history is not public. An itemised record of where you have been and when is not something we publish about you.
  • Location is used for two things only: showing shops near you, and confirming you are physically at a shop when you check in or scan.
  • Raw location is kept only as long as abuse prevention requires, then deleted on a schedule. We do not maintain a permanent movement archive.
  • Leaderboards show a town or city, never an address. If you would rather not appear at that level of detail, you can set a broader home base or opt out of local boards.

5. PhotographsNew

Dis-Go asks for live photographs in a small number of places. Every one of them passes through a review step before anyone other than you can see it, because a photograph of a shopfront can accidentally include a passer-by, a face through a window, or a vehicle registration plate.

  • Photographs must be taken in the app at the time. They cannot be chosen from your gallery.
  • Each submission is reviewed for incidentally captured people and private details before it becomes visible to anyone else.
  • Rejected photographs are deleted rather than kept.
  • Images captured solely to read a barcode are processed and discarded, not stored.
  • If a photograph of you has been published and you want it removed, write to us and we will remove it.

6. How we use your information

  • Account management: Creating, maintaining, and securing your account
  • Platform operation: Displaying posts, comments, and profiles; enabling forum interaction; showing shops, recording your collection, and calculating points
  • Safety and moderation: Detecting and acting on violations of our Terms of Service, including spam, harassment, and ban evasion
  • Abuse prevention: Detecting faked location, duplicate claims, and other attempts to obtain rewards that were not earned
  • Communications: Sending account-related notifications (verification, security alerts, moderation actions)
  • Platform improvement: Understanding how the platform is used to make it better
  • Legal compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable law, including DMCA takedown processing

We do not use your data for advertising, selling to data brokers, or any purpose unrelated to operating this platform.

7. What we will not doNew

Commitments, not aspirations
  • We will not sell or rent your personal data
  • We will not show your precise location to another user
  • We will not publish your check-in history
  • We will not build private messaging between users, and if that ever changes we will announce it rather than ship it quietly
  • We will not use your data to target advertising
  • We will not share your collection or activity with a record shop as personally identifiable information without your consent

8. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Third-party vendors who assist in operating the platform (hosting, database, authentication, email and SMS delivery) under confidentiality obligations
  • Record shops, in aggregate only: A verified shop may see counts and trends for its own listing. It does not receive your identity, your location, or your collection.
  • Payment processors: If you make a purchase, your payment information is handled directly by our PCI-compliant payment provider and is never stored by us
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of The Peripheral Initiative, its users, or the public
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may transfer to the new entity, with notice provided to users

Your username and public posts are visible to all platform visitors by default, unless you adjust your privacy settings. In Dis-Go, your username, rank and point total are visible on leaderboards.

10. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data
  • Right to portability: Request your data, including your collection, in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to certain uses of your data
  • Right to restriction: Request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at lachlan@theperipheralinitiative.com. We will respond within 30 days. Account deletion can also be initiated directly through your account Settings at any time.

One limit, stated plainly. When an account is deleted or banned we keep a one-way hash of the username and contact method. We cannot reverse it into your email address or phone number, and it exists only so that a removed account cannot immediately return. Everything else associated with the account is deleted.

Users in the European Union or California may have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively. Full regional compliance details will be addressed in the legally reviewed version of this Policy.

11. Cookies and local storage

The website uses browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to maintain your session state, preferences, and locally stored activity such as votes and saves. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

We may use session cookies for security purposes (for example, CSRF protection). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies that report your behaviour to external services.

You can clear your local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will sign you out and reset your locally stored preferences.

13. Children's privacy

The Platform and Dis-Go are intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 18 has registered, please contact us at lachlan@theperipheralinitiative.com immediately.

14. Data security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Hashed storage of sensitive verification data — passwords and answer keys are never stored in plaintext
  • Access controls limiting who within our organization can access personal data
  • Server-side enforcement of scoring and permissions, so they cannot be altered from a device
  • Regular review of our security practices

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Security incidentsNew

If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify affected users and any regulator we are required to inform, within the timeframes applicable law requires. Our notice will describe what happened, what data was involved, what we have done, and what you can do. We maintain an internal incident response process for this purpose.
