The app

Own the music.Don't rent it.

Dis-Go turns record shops into a map. Walk in, scan what you find, and keep a collection that stays yours whether or not anyone's licensing deal survives the year.

Built for electronic music, open to everything. Points come from what a record actually is — the format, the pressing, how far it travelled to reach that shelf.

How it works

Three things, in order

The order matters, which is the whole point — you can't skip to the end from your sofa.

1
Go somewhere
Shops appear on a map. Checking in needs your actual location, confirmed by GPS, because the record you're standing in front of is the record that counts.
2
Scan what you find
Point the camera at a barcode. Dis-Go reads the release — artist, format, pressing, whether it was pressed for the market you're standing in — and scores it.
3
Keep it
Your collection is a record of places you went and things you held. Browsing without buying counts. That's what record shops are for.

Dis-Go is in development

There's no download link on this page because there's nothing to download. The design is finished — every screen has been built and tested — and the app itself is being built now, for iPhone and Android together.

The forum is live today, and it's where this gets figured out in the open. If you collect physical music, or you run a shop that sells it, that conversation is the useful place to be.
