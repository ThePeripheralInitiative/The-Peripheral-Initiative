Who We Are
About Us

The Peripheral Initiative is a collective of music ideologies that exists on the edge of how music is discovered, and how it is supposed to be heard. Your peripheral vision senses movement before your eyes ever confirm it: a presence, a shift, something real before it has a shape. This is built the same way. There's no single voice behind it. There doesn't need to be.

The Peripheral Initiative is driven by the independence and freedom at the heart of EDM. By giving users a platform to build community, discover new music, and connect with one another, it offers Independent Artists and Labels an EDM-centric space built around their courage and freedom of expression. Without artistic expression, there is no creativity: no exploration.
