6. Dis-Go — using the appNew

6.1 What Dis-Go is

Dis-Go is a discovery application for physical music. It shows record and media shops on a map, confirms you are physically present when you check in, and lets you scan items to build a collection. It awards points based on format, pressing and other release attributes.

Dis-Go does not sell music and contains no marketplace. There is no way to purchase a release through the app, by design.

6.2 Your safety is your judgment

Dis-Go directs you toward real physical locations, some of which will be submitted by other users. Only visit or add shops in public, well-lit places, and never enter a location that feels unsafe. You are responsible for your own safety, your awareness of your surroundings, and compliance with local law while using the app. Never trespass, and never enter private areas of a business.

6.3 Accuracy of shop information

Shop listings in Dis-Go are built from publicly available data and from user submissions. When you contribute to a listing you agree that:

  • You will only add a shop that genuinely exists, from a public vantage point
  • You will only report a shop as closed or moved if you personally observed that
  • You will not submit false information about a business, including its stock, hours or status
  • You will not report a shop as closed, unsafe, or otherwise problematic where you hold a competing commercial interest
  • You will only submit photographs you took yourself, of the exterior, avoiding identifiable people and vehicle registration plates

Closure reports require independent confirmation from multiple separate accounts and moderator review before any listing status changes.

6.4 Fair play

The following are prohibited and may result in loss of points, loss of standing, or termination:

  • Falsifying your location, including GPS spoofing, mock location tools, emulators or modified clients
  • Automating any part of the app, including scripted scanning or check-ins
  • Claiming a purchase you did not make in order to obtain a shop's rewards
  • Using additional accounts to gain any advantage
  • Interfering with the scoring, reporting or verification systems
Visiting a shop without buying anything is entirely permitted and always earns points in the normal way. Only a shop's own rewards require a verified purchase, because those are funded by that shop.

6.5 Points, ranks and rewards have no monetary value

Points, levels, ranks, badges and standing at a shop are not currency and have no cash value. They cannot be purchased, sold, transferred between accounts, exchanged for money, or redeemed against anything except where a shop chooses to offer a reward.

Where a shop offers a reward, that reward is the shop's, not ours. The shop sets its own thresholds and terms and is responsible for honouring them. We do not fund, guarantee, or underwrite any shop's reward, and we are not a party to it.

We may adjust, recalculate or remove points obtained in breach of these Terms.

7. Shop listings and businessesNew

Dis-Go includes listings for real businesses. Listings may exist before a business is aware of them, because they are built from publicly available information and user contributions.

  • If you operate a listed business, you may claim your listing, correct its details, or ask us to remove it. Write to lachlan@theperipheralinitiative.com and we will respond promptly.
  • Claiming requires verification that you are authorised to act for the business. Different levels of verification unlock different levels of control, and anything that creates value requires the strongest level.
  • Stock information is labelled by its source — community-reported, confirmed by the shop, or synchronised from the shop's own system — so no listing implies a level of accuracy it does not have.
  • A shop cannot moderate its own discussion thread. Verified shops may post with a badge and may report content, but cannot remove messages about them. The value of a shop thread to users depends on it being candid.
  • We will correct or remove inaccurate information about a business on request, and we will investigate reports of coordinated or malicious reporting against a listing.
